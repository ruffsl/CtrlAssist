# Wheels, flight sticks and levers

The control space is open-ended and groups can be declared, so such pads are representable. Not modeled: a lever with no rest position counts as touched forever, and position-dependent force feedback (spring, damper) can't be reduced to a level. Legacy issue #53 also asks for button debounce.

**Why deferred.** No such pad is on hand to verify against.

**Would bring it in.** Someone with the hardware who can test.
