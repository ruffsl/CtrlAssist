# A cookbook of arrangements

The 0.4.0 README had a Cookbook: two controllers as one hand each, like split Joy-Cons; a standard gamepad with an Xbox Adaptive Controller; and two recipes that chained several running commands, a mux's virtual pad read by a demux and back. "Couch Co-Op Swap" and "Double Agent Tag Team" are in that README, at the `v0.4.0` tag. A page of such recipes in the user guide would show what the full form of a profile is for.

**Why deferred.** None of the recipes has been tried on the rewrite. A profile's full form describes a whole graph in one file, which should replace chaining commands; where a recipe really wants one setup's virtual pad as another's input, it isn't known whether a daemon takes its own virtual pad for a pad to play with, or should. A recipe in the guide has to have been played.

**Would bring it in.** Trying the two recipes as profiles once the user guide's reshaping is done: each written in the full form, started, and played. What works becomes a Cookbook page under Using CtrlAssist; what doesn't becomes a concern or an idea of its own.
