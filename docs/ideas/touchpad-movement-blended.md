# Two touchpads blended by their fingers' movement

In average, a virtual pad's touchpad is whichever pad's touched first, until that pad lifts: two fingers' places have no mean that says anything, and a place between them jumps as the second finger lands and lifts. What can be blended is movement: one finger of the mux's own, put down while either pad is touched, that moves by the mean of what the two fingers moved since the last step, as two sticks averaged move a pointer.

**Why deferred.** The mux would keep a place of its own between steps, where everything it puts out now is some input's value or a mean of them. That place drifts from where either finger is, so it is wrong for a game that reads the touchpad by region, a half for each of two buttons or the screen mapped onto it, and it has to be held at the touchpad's edges. Nobody has asked to share a touchpad that way.

**Would bring it in.** People in average who use the touchpad as a pointer together, in a game or on the desktop, and find taking turns at it a loss.
