# A choice's own sentence, where it is chosen

Every choice the daemon serves for a preset's fields carries a sentence on what choosing it does: "Two pads as one. The assist takes over whatever it touches." The window showed the chosen one's sentence under its dropdown until the panes were rebuilt on TisVlay's rows, which have no such line. It could come back in the dropdown's open list, each choice with its sentence under its label, or in the tray's menu as a tooltip.

**Why deferred.** The select widget lists labels only, and it is TisVlay's: the change is one to make there too. The group's "i" and the book cover the presets meanwhile.

**Would bring it in.** People picking a preset or an identity without knowing what it does, or the select gaining a second line for some other reason.
