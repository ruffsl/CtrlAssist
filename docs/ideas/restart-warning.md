# Saying that a game may need restarting

A change that replaces a setup's virtual pad, as another identity does, or a stop and a start, is to a game its controller unplugged and another plugged in. Some games take the new one up in part: on 2026-11-22 Hades, streamed through Moonshine, went on taking buttons and sticks from a virtual DualSense that had been replaced three times, and stopped sending it rumble until the game was restarted. The tray's "restarted" notification says a game saw its controller disconnect and another connect; it could go on to say that a game which has gone quiet on rumble or lights wants restarting. So could the window, where a change that costs a restart is confirmed.

**Why deferred.** One game, once, and the restarts were a test's, three in a few minutes: it isn't known whether it was the game, Steam's own handling of controllers between the two, or the streaming host. A warning about what games do should say something true of more than one.

**Would bring it in.** The same from another game or outside a stream; or finding which layer drops the rumble, which may say there is something the daemon could do instead, such as keeping the virtual pad through more kinds of change.
