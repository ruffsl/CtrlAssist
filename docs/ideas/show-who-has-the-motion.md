# Showing who has the motion

The tray and the window say who has control in a toggle. Nothing says whose motion sensors the game has: the primary's, or the assist's while they hold the button that takes them. The input thread would report a change of who has the motion as it reports a selection, and the tray and the window would show it beside who has control.

**Why deferred.** Unlike a selection, it changes for as long as a button is held, many times a minute, and the one who changed it has their thumb on the button. People who share a controller do ask to see what the helper is doing: a study of fourteen of them found that not knowing who has which control "can lead to confusion and frustration" ([Ahmetovic et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.02132v1)).

**Would bring it in.** A primary player surprised by an aim that moved without them, or a wish for it from someone who plays this way.
